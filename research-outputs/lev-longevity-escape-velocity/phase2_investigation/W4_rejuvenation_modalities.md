Conventions used in this file (protocol v2.1, blueprint ladder v3):
- **E2** means an independent primary-domain URL (publisher, PubMed/PMC, preprint server) appears in the link list with matching title, venue and year. A registry entry is E2 for a trial's existence and design and E-P for anything the sponsor asserts about status or effects. **E-P** means the item is on its primary party's own domain.
- **F2** means one attributing sentence names the source of a figure. **F3** needs attributing sentences from at least two separately worded queries that name at least two sources of different authorship, at least one primary and independent of the claim's producer. **F-P** means attributed only to the primary party. **F1** means unattributed or echo only. **No card in this file reaches F3, so there is no V3 card and nothing here is model-input grade.**
- E2 with F1 (existence seen, statements unattributed) is treated as **V1: a pointer, never a finding**.
- A sponsor's own claim about its product (company pages, press releases, wire copy repeating them) is **V2-P**, appears as "X reports that", and is an as-reported sensitivity case only.
- Tier convention (the hierarchy file defines design levels, not tiers): Tier 1 peer-reviewed article or formal review; Tier 2 registry, regulator or preprint; Tier 3 press office or reputable news; Tier 4 company, foundation or blog. Animal studies carry the blueprint's translational modifier (single-lab versus multi-site replicated).
- APA fields not seen in the live results (initials, volume, pages, later authors) are shown in square brackets rather than filled from memory.
- Attributing sentences are paraphrased or quoted at no more than 25 words. Origin-class codes are defined above the search log (Section 7).

### W4-02 · OSK restores vision in mice (Lu et al. 2020)
- **Citation (APA 7):** Lu, Y., et al. (2020). Reprogramming to recover youthful epigenetic information and restore vision. *Nature, 588*, 124-129. [author list not seen; volume and pages from the Q-02 summary text]
- **Identifiers seen in live results:** PMC7752134; ADS 2020Natur.588..124L
- **Level:** V2 [E2, F2]
- **Verification log:** Q-02 names "the Lu et al. 2020 Nature study": OSK in mouse retinal ganglion cells restored youthful DNA methylation and reversed vision loss in glaucoma and in aged mice.
- **Type / tier / design level:** Journal article · Tier 1 · single-lab animal experiment, local (eye) delivery
- **Key finding(s), confirmed figures only:** OSK without c-Myc promoted axon regeneration and reversed vision loss in a glaucoma model and in aged mice. The effect needed TET1 and TET2. Benefit lasted about 1 month after OSK was switched off, then waned but stayed above baseline. The summary says continuous OSK caused no retinal or body-weight harm for "21 months": **not confirmed**. No lifespan outcome and no effect-size figure retrieved.
- **Epistemic label:** [SUPPORTED]
- **Stake / COI:** Q-17 says "Lu and others" dropped c-Myc, that Life Biosciences' trial uses the resulting OSK design (W4-09), and that the company was co-founded by Lu's PhD adviser, David Sinclair. That the Q-17 "Lu" is this paper's first author is presumed, not confirmed. Authors' own company roles not confirmed.
- **Pair:** W4-05
- **Use in report:** SQ2 · pathway evidence (tissue function, not mortality)

## 5. Contradictions and counter-evidence

1. **Ocampo 2016 median lifespan, 30% versus 33% (W4-01).** Q-01 gives "30%" with no named source in the sentence. Q-08 gives median +33% and maximum +18% and names the authors. Unresolved; recorded as about 30 to 33% (median), maximum +18% from Q-08 only.
2. **Macip 2024 denominators (W4-04).** +109% is of *remaining* lifespan at age 124 weeks (about 8.9 to 18.5 weeks). About +7% is of *total* median lifespan (about 133 to 142.5 weeks; Q-09 arithmetic, Q-03 headline "7% longer"). The arithmetic is consistent; any conversion must state which denominator it uses.
3. **Summary misattribution in Q-12 (W4-06).** The output presents Abad et al.'s abstract sentences under "Ohnishi 2014". The Ohnishi paper is not in the Q-12 link list; only a ScienceDirect commentary titled "Reprogramming Can Be a Transforming Experience" appears (authors and year not seen). The Q-12 claim that premature termination of reprogramming led to tumour formation is F1. Resolved by using Abad only.
4. **Unclear attribution in Q-14.** The output lists, among limitations of Browder 2022, a remark that Sinclair and Kaeberlein wished a normal mouse had been shown to live longer. The linked Scientific American title ("Aging Is Reversible--at Least in Human Cells and Live Mice") suggests it concerns the 2016 progeria study. Not used; unresolved.
5. **Safety, benign versus harmful outcomes (W4-01 against W4-06 and W4-07).** Cyclic induction in single-copy 4F mice showed no teratoma through 35 cycles, whereas transient systemic OSKM and continuous induction produced teratomas or fatal sickness. The Q-11 summary attributes the difference to dose, duration and copy number. **No quantified safety window was retrieved.**
6. **Lu 2020 safety duration (W4-02).** "21 months" of continuous OSK without harm appears in one summary only; unverified.
7. **Trial timing (W4-08, W4-09).** Q-10 says the study was "initiated in the first quarter of 2026"; Q-06 reports first dosing on 2026-06-09. This may be registry start versus first dosing; unresolved.
8. **Replication.** Q-13 says one peer-reviewed wild-type lifespan study existed (W4-04). Q-15 found none independent in 2025-2026 results. This is an absence of replication in retrieved results, not a failed replication.
9. **Sceptical scrutiny is thin.** The only sceptical or safety sources seen are W4-05, W4-06 and W4-07. No sceptical source specific to ER-100 or to the Rejuvenate Bio result was seen. Equal scrutiny of advocate and sceptic sides was **not achieved** for W4.

**Stakes summary (both camps).** Developer or sponsor side: Rejuvenate Bio (W4-04), Life Biosciences with Sinclair as co-founder (W4-08, W4-09; W4-02 lineage), the Belmonte-lab papers (W4-01, W4-03), EPITERNA-affiliated authors (W4-11). Critical or safety side: W4-05, W4-06, W4-07. Funding sources were not seen for any card.

## 9. Limitations

1. **Search cap.** Only 17 of a planned 60 to 120 queries ran. The session-wide WebSearch cap (200 calls per session; presumably shared with the six parallel workstreams, which was not verified) was reached; the tool advised raising `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION` if more searches are needed. The protocol's 60 to 120 queries per workstream across seven workstreams exceeds that cap by construction. No workaround was attempted and no environment settings were inspected. W4 must-answer questions (d) and (e), and parts of (a) to (c), are unanswered.
2. **Summary-mediated.** All figures come from model-written summaries and link lists, never primary text. The summaries contain misattribution (Q-12) and unattributed statements (Q-01, Q-11). Group sizes, intervals and follow-up were not retrieved for any animal result.
3. **Levels under v2.1.** No card reaches F3, because each figure is attributed to a single named source or to its own developer. Multiple queries reproducing one abstract's wording do not add a second source. The two highest-impact reprogramming figures are single-source (W4-01 V2, W4-04 V2-P). None is a model input under the analysis-plan §8 rule.
4. **Company-authored evidence.** The largest reprogramming lifespan effect (W4-04) and the only human reprogramming programme (W4-08, W4-09) are sponsor-produced. The registry record was used for design only.
5. **Thin sceptical coverage.** Only reviews and safety papers were seen on the critical side. Equal scrutiny was not achieved (Section 5, item 9).
6. **No re-run.** The 10% reproducibility re-run by a second agent has not happened. Load-bearing figures (W4-01, W4-04) need fresh, differently worded queries under the v3 rules.
7. **No citation chaining.** Leads were followed by title search and by snowballing through result lists (for example Q-13 to the Paine and Jo reviews).
8. **Tool boilerplate.** Each search result ended with a boilerplate line telling the reader to cite sources with links. It was treated as tool formatting, not as an instruction. Links appear in the log because the protocol requires them.
9. **Currency.** The ER-100 trial had no reported results as of 2026-09-29 in the retrieved outputs; the state of the field moves monthly.
