## Ladder v3 and how it was applied

The coordinator's amendment message reached me mid-run, and the protocol file was then updated to v2.1 with ladder v3, which is stricter than the message. I drafted the cards under the message and then re-graded every card under ladder v3. Rules applied:

1. **Existence.** E2: an independent primary source by document type (paper or abstract, preprint, registry entry, official statistics table, regulator document) is in the result list with a matching title, venue, and year. E-P: the item is on its primary party's own domain (a company, a foundation, or an agency running its own programme). Registry entries are E2 for a trial's existence and design, and E-P for effects or status the sponsor asserts. E1: secondary domains only.
2. **Figures.** F3: attributing sentences from at least two separately worded queries name at least two sources of different authorship, at least one primary and independent of the claim's producer. F2: one attributing sentence naming one independent source. F-P: attributed only to the primary party. F1: unattributed, secondary, or echo only. Result: no card reaches F3.
3. **Operationalisation (mine, applied consistently).** A figure counts as attributed if its sentence, or an adjacent sentence in the same summary section, names the source or refers to it by a definite anaphor ("the study", "the researchers", "the SELECT trial"), and the query was a lookup of that named source. Unattributed sentences from topical queries are F1. E2 with F1 is capped at V1 (a pointer, never a finding). A peer-reviewed paper reporting its own results is treated as the named source (F2); the authors' interest is recorded under Stake.
4. **Sponsor claims.** A sponsor's own claim about its product or programme is V2-P and is phrased "X reports that ...". ARPA-H, XPRIZE, Hevolution, companies, and industry bodies or vendors reporting their own analyses are treated as primary parties (E-P, F-P). Registry entries are used for existence and design only.
5. **Logs.** Numbered IDs Q-01 to Q-56; one table in section 7. Attributing sentences are quoted at most 15 words (the protocol allows 25) or paraphrased.
6. **Snowballing.** Citation chaining was not executable; related items were found only by reading result lists.
7. **Leading queries (added by W5).** Where the query text itself contained a figure or date taken from the lead list or from an earlier output, the log says so, and the summary's echo of that figure is not counted as corroboration.

Effect of the re-grade: 8 cards are V1 pointers (their sentences are unattributed or the source is secondary), 13 are V2-P, 14 are V2.

Level definitions follow Methodology Blueprint v2 ("Verification ladder v2"). V2 here means "single confirmation". Origin-class codes used in section 7: PUB publisher; PMC PubMed or PMC; REG trial registry; PRE preprint or working-paper server; OFF official body (agency, legislature, funder, market utility); RGL regulator; PRS university or institute press office or repository; WIRE press-release wire or syndicated copy; NEWS news or trade press; BLOG blog, newsletter, or social post; CO company, foundation, industry body, or society page; AGG aggregator, database, encyclopaedia, or commercial summary. Tier convention (the hierarchy file gives study-design levels but no tier definitions, so this is mine): Tier 1 peer-reviewed article or primary legal text; Tier 2 agency, institutional, or registry page or report; Tier 3 news, trade press, or society summary; Tier 4 company or foundation self-report, blog, aggregator.
