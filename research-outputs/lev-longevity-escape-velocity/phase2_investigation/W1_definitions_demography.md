### (a) Origin, wording, earlier terms, variants

- **The 2004 article is described as introducing "actuarial escape velocity" (AEV).** Q-06 and Q-08 describe de Grey (2004, W1-01) as introducing the term, as a rate of reduction of age-specific mortality risk, with a remaining-life-expectancy analogy (a fall with upward thrust). The full text was not reachable (Q-09). No primary-domain result dates an earlier use of the term.
- **The RQ Brief's assumption is not supported by anything seen.** The Brief says "actuarial escape velocity" is an earlier term "credited to others". Only encyclopaedia and blog summaries credit David Gobel (Methuselah Foundation) with conceiving the concept or the phrase in the early 2000s (Q-02, Q-03, Q-07, Q-12), and cite a 1978 Robert Anton Wilson essay as a precursor idea (Q-05). These are E1-only, and the summaries disagree with each other (section 5, C1 to C2). Suggested safe wording: "AEV is described as introduced in de Grey (2004); earlier attributions rest on secondary summaries."
- **The exact phrase "longevity escape velocity" has no dated first use here.** The 2004 title contains "Escape velocity"; the phrase "longevity escape velocity" was not seen in that article. A lexicographer's post on the phrase (Q-11 link) may date it; it was not read.
- **Peer-reviewed formalisation:** Debonneuil, Loisel and Planchet (2018, W1-02) is the only one seen. **Critiques of LEV as a concept:** none read; candidate papers are in section 6-B. **LEV Foundation's own wording:** not returned (Q-03 gave no foundation page).

## 5. Contradictions and counter-evidence

- **C1 · Origin of AEV / LEV (W1-01; Q-02, Q-05, Q-06, Q-08, Q-12).** Q-06 and Q-08 say the 2004 article introduces AEV. Q-02 says the term was "coined by de Grey in a 2004 paper" and was "first articulated" by Gobel in the early 2000s, and in the same passage speaks of de Grey's "earlier use" of AEV "in a 2004 analysis" (earlier and 2004 in one sentence). Q-05 places the idea in a 1978 Wilson essay. Unresolved: no primary text was reachable (Q-09). The RQ Brief's "earlier term credited to others" is not supported by anything seen.
- **C2 · Who coined "longevity escape velocity" (Q-07 versus Q-12).** Q-07: de Grey "formalized and coined" the term in academic literature, Gobel originated the concept. Q-12: de Grey coined "escape velocity" in 2004, and Gobel coined "Longevity Escape Velocity" while working with de Grey and Diamandis on prizes. Unresolved; both rest on encyclopaedia or blog origins.
- **C3 · Required decline: "4%" versus plan P2 (W1-02).** Debonneuil et al. report LEV under "sustained mortality improvements of 4%" (Q-11, Q-14); the frozen plan's P2 gives 6.7% to 9.4% per year for D-N at MRDT 10 to 7 years.
