## 6. Discrepancies to be aware of
1. Ollie Sr.'s middle initial: C. (1979 obituary) vs H. (2021 obituary of son Michael).
2. Larry and Jerry Price are called twins, but their obituaries print birth years 1954 and 1955. Bruce was born Nov 1955, so the twins were almost certainly born 30 Apr 1954.
3. Sadie Mae (Thurston) Reynolds: born 1889 per obituary, 1894 per gravestone.
4. Ida May (Birdnow) Myers: married 1883 per husband's obituary, but born 1874 per gravestone.
5. John Lewis Hunter's birth year appears as 1885, 1886 and 1888 in different records.
6. The Calhoun County veteran list calls Howard James Price a Spanish-American War veteran but gives a "Gas Detachment" unit, which is a World War I formation.

## 7. Where to go next (all free unless noted)
- **FamilySearch.org** (free account): 1900–1950 federal censuses. Priority searches: Clowers household, Calhoun Co. 1900 (John Lewis Hunter's mother); John W. Buchanan household, Calhoun Co. 1900 (Rosa); Howard Price household, Missouri 1910 and Hardin 1920 (Anna Sloan, Ollie as a child); George E. Myers household, Jersey/Madison Co. 1940 and 1950 (Shirley as a child, Mabel's birthplace); Charles C. Reynolds household, Greene/Jersey Co. 1930–1950 (Bill as a child).
- **Illinois Statewide Death Index 1916–1950** and **Illinois Statewide Marriage Index 1763–1900** (ilsos.gov, free): Hunter–Buchanan 1908 is already indexed; look for Price–Sloan (may be Missouri), Myers–Jones (c. 1925–1932), Reynolds–Myers (c. 1950s), Reynolds–Thurston (c. 1905–1911, Greene Co.).
- **Jersey County Historical Society**, 601 N. State St., Jerseyville (Thu 1–4, Fri–Sat 10–4): funeral-home records, cemetery lists and newspaper files for Jersey and Greene counties; the best place to get Shirley (Myers) Reynolds's 2019–2021 obituary and George and Mabel Myers's obituaries, which were not online.
- **Calhoun County** records are held at the IRAD depository, Western Illinois University, Macomb; the Calhoun ILGenWeb site (calhoun.illinoisgenweb.org) already has the Hunter descendancy PDF, cemetery and marriage transcriptions used here.
- **Find a Grave**: memorials exist for John Louis Hunter (no. 162831933, with the 1945 Telegraph obituary), Hardin City Cemetery (Howard Price), Carter Cemetery (Charles and Sadie Reynolds), Hartford Cemetery (John H. and Ida Myers, Birdnows), Oak Grove Cemetery Jerseyville (Larry, Jerry, Gilbert and Raymond Reynolds). The site blocked automated access, so these were cross-checked through ILGenWeb transcriptions instead.
- **Crawford Funeral Home, Jerseyville** (crawfordfunerals.com) holds the full online obituaries for Ollie Price III, David E. Price, Bruce Audie Price, Evelyn Morris and Virginia (Kuehnel) Myers; **Alexander & Gubser Funeral Home** holds Pastor Clyde Myers's. Both sites refused automated fetches, so sibling lists were taken from mirrored copies and search extracts.
