Report reviewed: `phase4_report_draft.md` (deep-research `full` mode). Scope reference: `phase1_scoping.md`. Citation existence and anchors were checked deterministically upstream and are not re-examined here. Word counts below were measured after stripping the machine citation markers; body (title through 6.3 Closing) is 10,150 words, of which about 1,060 are table cells. ### Overall Assessment  (Verdict: Minor Revision; Weighted Score 3.85 / 5.0, rounds to 3.9)

The report answers the primary research question and all three sub-questions, keeps the four construct families apart as Checkpoint 1 required, pairs every well-known claim with its replication record, and states the sixth-sense verdict with the proponent replies before the Conclusion. Strength labels are carried from Findings into Discussion and Conclusion without upgrade. The reasons it is not an Accept are not scientific. The body is 27 percent over the mode's ceiling, the same evidence is re-narrated in up to six places, three cross-references point to the wrong section, one sentence in 5.1 contradicts the Findings' own level counts, and two words in the Conclusion ("frequently accurate", "often accurate") make a frequency claim the Findings never estimate. All of these are fixable without new sources or new analysis, which is why the verdict is Minor rather than Major Revision.

It is what lets Grove et al. (2000) and Kuncel et al. (2013) bound deliberate holistic judgment without being read as evidence against gut feelings, and it is applied consistently at every later use, including Table 3 rows 6 and 7 and the 5.2 decision-policy recommendation.

(2015) and Lejarraga and Hertwig (2021), and then 5.1 gives the sceptic's simpler story equal statement and admits the corpus cannot distinguish the two. ### Summary

This is a well-constructed synthesis that does what the Phase 1 brief and both devil's-advocate checkpoints asked of it. It answers the primary question with a single, honestly labelled framework (learning under valid feedback in a regular environment, with the present case belonging to that history), it answers sub-question 1 construct by construct, sub-question 2 with the pro-intuition thinness stated plainly, and sub-question 3 with a scoped verdict that puts the proponents' replies on the record before answering them. The construct-separation table, the symmetric treatment of unreplicated results in both directions, and the report's willingness to raise the circularity objection against its own best account are the reasons the scientific dimensions all score 4.
