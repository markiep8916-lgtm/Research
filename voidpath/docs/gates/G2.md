# Gate G2: prologue and chapter 1

Director's verdict on the C-alpha sub-wave (C1 prologue, C2 Driftmarket, C3 Shoals and Meridian,
CA-alpha, C9-alpha, C10-alpha), written from two reviews played on the build that already carries
the C-alpha cross-file fixes (docs/gates/C-alpha-reports.md, last section):

- **Art direction**: contact sheets, in-game lint, battles, bosses and key scenes at 1280x720 high and
  390x844 medium. Evidence in `shots/G2/art/` (`contact/`, `battles/`, `battles-phone/`, `boss-maw/`,
  `boss-sentinel/`, `powered/`, `enemies-phone/`, `scenes-phone/`, `crops/`, `ingame-lint.log`).
- **Game direction**: five human-pace runs (keys, a reading clock per box, the human-like battle
  policy with thinking time) and the transcripts. Evidence in `shots/G2/design/` (`run/`, `run-tail/`,
  `run-ch1/`, `run-ch1tail/`, `run-coil/`, `varo/`) and its tools.

## Verdict: FAIL

The bar is half met. The writing, Driftmarket, the Halcyon's rooms, the battle UI and the flow are at
the bar: every STORY beat and fixed line lands, the Varo log and "It's Nyx" work, the town is
charming, there are no softlocks, no wrong music, `missingArt()` is empty and every render budget
holds. Three things fail, and each would sink a chapter on its own:

1. **Encounters and levels.** A first-time player gets about half the planned fights (4 of 8 in the
   prologue, 4 of 10-12 in chapter 1). They reach the SENTINEL at level 4 (0% win in the sim) and
   the Maw at level 9 (8%), so both bosses are walls. `balance --check` passes only because
   `tests/campaign.mjs` assumes fight counts the maps never deliver.
2. **The chapter 1 dungeon is not readable.** The Meridian is near black at every viewpoint, powered
   or not, on both sizes. The Shoals stamp one bow-tie motif across every floor, the ice bridges are
   flat wallpaper over a starfield box, and the phone frames are mostly black.
3. **Story staging runs at about 1.5 times its plan.** The prologue's critical-path story takes 424 s
   against a 331 s plan, chapter 1's 552 s against 358. The extra time is staging awaited in
   series, not text. The chapter totals land in range only because the missing fights cancel out
   the extra story time.

There are also specific failures: the cold open fails visualLint, both Moths are crate piles, WARDEN's
first speech has no sigil screens, the SENTINEL has no mid-battle beat, the Maw sits out of focus,
the void eel vanishes on its own floor and never dives before the Maw does, and the Varo log plays
to Nyx's back.

**What happens now.** C-beta starts under the bar at the end of this file. The C-alpha owners fix the
items below beside C-beta, file-disjoint. C9 and C10 fold theirs into their beta tasks. System items go
to one G2-systems task that runs beside C-beta. Items it does not finish pass to the Wave I owner
named in brackets. Order matters: **W-1, T-1, C1-5 and C1-8 land first**, because other fixes and
C-beta's tuning depend on them. G3 re-runs the prologue and chapter 1 at human pace, re-shoots both
contact sheets, and judges every item below again.

### How these items are measured

- **Luma.** "Mean luma" is the mean 8-bit luma of the whole frame (PIL `convert('L')`), and
  "near-black" is the share of pixels with luma below 20. Both reviews' numbers use this measure:
  ```
  python3 -c "import sys;from PIL import Image;h=Image.open(sys.argv[1]).convert('L').histogram();n=sum(h);print(round(sum(i*c for i,c in enumerate(h))/n,1),round(100*sum(h[:20])/n),'%')" shot.png
  ```
  Reference values: the Halcyon cryo phone frame is 32.6 with 49% near-black. Every passing Halcyon
  and Driftmarket viewpoint sits at or above **mean 25 with at most 65% near-black on the phone**
  (390x844 medium) and **mean 40 with at most 50% near-black on desktop** (1280x720 high). Every
  Shoals and Meridian viewpoint the art review found too dark misses these floors. They are the
  minimum, not the target.
- **Human pace.** Run `node shots/G2/design/human-pace.mjs --start <jump> --out <dir>`, then
  `node shots/G2/design/analyze.mjs <dir>` for minutes per chapter, story seconds per scene, fights
  and levels. T-1 moves the tool into `tools/`.
- **Expected fights.** `RATES='{"zone":{"grace":g,"sigma":s}}' node shots/G2/design/tools/expected-fights.mjs`
  measures the critical-path zone distance and gives the expected random fights under explore.js's rules.
- **Boss by level.** `node shots/G2/design/tools/boss-by-level.mjs` gives the win rate per level.
- **Story time** is cutscene time minus nested battles, with each box read for 1.2 s + 1 s per 18
  characters (the scriptcheck reading model). The target for each critical-path scene is its
  WRITING 5.2/5.3 plan **+15%**.

## Bar for C-beta and C-gamma

C-alpha's writing, staging ideas, town density and battle UI set the bar. Its economy, its timing,
and its look outside the Halcyon and the town do not. Every rule below comes from something that
failed at G2. G3 judges C-beta against these rules and against 11.1-11.8.

### The bar to copy

- **Look**: the Halcyon's cryo deck, engineering and the new reactor control room, and Driftmarket's
  docks and inn. The control room's coloured screen lighting is the model for any room with
  consoles. **Do not copy the Shoals, the Meridian or the exterior.**
- **Writing**: one voice, funny from character, moving without speeches. The Varo log is 5 boxes,
  Ruse's seeding 4, HALCYON's fragment 6. "She looks tired. The stories never said she looked
  tired." Tobin's beacon pays off a prologue terminal.
- **Town**: Driftmarket's 14 looks with walk cycles and portraits, each with a 2-3 box beat, and a
  favours board that pays off on other maps.
- **Battle**: the telegraph banners, the SENTINEL's lock-on reticle and defeat blowouts, the Maw's
  submerge glow and sinking ring, and the timing of the awakening cut-in.

### Measure before you hand in

1. **Play your chapter at human pace** (T-1, or the G2 driver with your route). It must land inside
   11.7 both at the planned level and at the levels your critical path actually delivers. Report
   `debug.pacing()`, the story seconds of each scene, the fights and the level at the boss.
2. **Fights come from distance.** Measure your critical-path zone distance with expected-fights.
   11.7 asks for 300-380 units in chapters 2-4. Set `REG.zoneRates` so the expected random fights
   match the 11.7 count. Prefer building the distance to raising the rates: below about grace 6 and
   sigma 10, fights come back to back. C10's route uses the measured count, never a hand-set
   `fights:`.
3. **Check the phone first, at full size.** Every viewpoint must meet the luma floor (phone: mean
   25 or more, at most 65% near-black; desktop: mean 40 or more, at most 50%). Aim for the Halcyon
   cryo deck (33 and 49% on a phone). A "dark" place is a mood made with contrast and pools of light,
   not a black frame. Run `debug.visualLint` at every viewpoint in the real game page. Passing lint
   does not mean a frame is readable: the Meridian passed lint at about 10 luma.
4. **Check every boss with a first-timer's gear** (C10-3), not only with the sim's route.

### Pacing, fights and flow

5. **Story seconds include staging.** C-alpha's plans were exceeded by 50% because pans, gathers,
   walk-ins and fades were awaited one after another. Follow WRITING 1.8 (staging seconds): moves and
   camera run under the lines, camera moves take about 1 s, and there is no wait padding. Each scene
   must land within its plan + 15% at human pace.
6. **Tips and lessons are deterministic.** No onboarding tip may hang on a random roll: put it on a
   scripted fight or a visible field encounter. Teach one new mechanic per fight. The enemy that
   teaches a boss mechanic uses it in round 1 or 2 by script (`chooseAction`), not by weight. The
   examples for C-beta:
   - the Spore Drone's sleep and the Feral Caretaker's summon (Arboretum);
   - the Trooper's mark and the Riot Drone's jam (Spire);
   - the Data Wraith's phase and the Corrupted Memory's Overwrite charge (Vault). The Glitch Swarm's
     shift needs a Break first, so put it in a scripted fight where a Break comes easily.
7. **Bosses run 8-10 rounds with at least 2 Breaks** against a first-timer's gear (the Warden
   10-14 rounds across both forms, as 10.4 sets).
   - Untargetable, phase and submerge rounds stay under 25% of the fight.
   - The awakening sits at the 50% beat.
   - Every boss has at least one mid-battle `say` or cue beat.
   - A break-cancel mechanic (the Gardener's Photosynthesis, Echo's Severance) must be cancellable in
     practice, not only in the sim.
8. **Signpost every new gear source and restock** with a toast, a shop line or a hub line. Put the
   chapter's best weapon where the player will equip it: chest gear offers Equip now (W-2), and
   until that lands, put it in the shop or have a line point to it.
9. **Retry is part of the flow.**
   - Log a checkpoint after any lock you open on the way to a boss.
   - Keep the pre-fight part of a scripted fight in the same outermost script as its `cs.battle`.
   - Do not rely on Retry keeping talk progress until F-1 lands.
10. **The Journal follows every puzzle step** (11.8): one objective per step for the Arboretum valves,
    the Spire grids and the Vault crystals (WRITING 9.3).

### Writing and staging

11. **No UI instructions in character lines.** The character says the in-world version, and
    `cs.caption` carries the menu path (WRITING 1.6). The prologue's equip tip is the pattern.
12. **Name things on screen before anyone refers to them.** A key-item toast is the cheapest way.
13. **Frame every reveal.** The subject is visible and not behind a traveler. Spread gathers so no
    speaker is covered. On a phone, speakers sit above the dialog box and inside the tilt band.
    Check the frame on a phone.
14. **WARDEN.** WARDEN always speaks over the gold sigil with gold light, never on a grey grade. The
    sigil appears only on maps that have `screen` (or pod status) props in the speaking frame
    (WRITING 1.7):
    - C4 needs them on the Choir pods for `arboretum.theo`;
    - C5's Spire propaganda screens are `screen` props, and `spire.screen` inspects them;
    - if a scene kills the lights, add a gold light source.
15. **Give every `scenes` entry a `jump` and `at`,** so the contact sheet frames the real staging.

### Art

16. **Never ship a 32 px tile whose detail is a single high-contrast blob** (snow patches, rime spots,
    stencils). It stamps across the frame, as the Shoals bow-ties, Meridian cap flowers and
    Driftmarket frost did. Use world-space noise, decals with random rotation and scale, or 3 or more
    variants through the legend's mix. Then look at a frame showing 20 or more cells of the same
    floor before calling it done.
17. **Pits, abysses, bridges and water need geometry and light**: thickness, rims, and a glow or fog
    from below. Never use a flat patterned rectangle or a starfield box. This applies to the
    Arboretum channels and walkways, the Vault's light bridges and the Heart's lifts.
18. **Hero props need real silhouettes,** not stacks of axis-aligned boxes. Every map has one lit,
    animated focal piece with particles, as the POC reactor has.
19. **Every viewpoint mixes warm and cold light**, with at least 3 coloured pools in frame and
    foreground silhouettes at the edges. Keep tiny, very bright emitters out of the defocus bands:
    until E-1 lands they turn into square bokeh.
20. **Bosses: the head or core stays inside the battle focus band** (`stage.slot`, `fitBox`). Check
    the card, the idle and one move frame at both sizes.
    - Bespoke arena floors must not read as holes.
    - Sheets taller than 160 px need irregular silhouettes on every edge.
    - The idle must visibly move at battle scale.
    - This matters most for the 256 px Warden and the Gardener.
21. **Regular enemies must contrast with their own arena floor** at phone size, next to the POC drone.
    A dark sprite on a dark floor of the same hue fails, as the void eel does on blue ice.
