Condensed final reports of the C-alpha tasks (CA-alpha, C9-alpha, C1, C2, C3, C10-alpha): deviations, cross-file requests and known issues.

## CA-alpha

CA-alpha is done. I painted all five regular enemies for the prologue and the Shoals with the POC enemy rig (shaded analytic shapes, one key light, part-separation lines, tinted outline, albedo and emissive painted together). They are registered through the enemy art registry under their binding art ids, which are the same as their kinds.

- **sentinel_mk1** (104x88, prologue/enemyart.js): a first-generation Halcyon Security patrol walker. It shares the Sentinel's family look (ivory plates, crimson livery, bronze horn crest, magenta visor) but has a new silhouette: reverse-jointed legs, a capacitor backpack with an amber sweeping beacon, and a rail cannon slung under the chest. Its `special` animation is the telegraphed charge: the coils blaze and light gathers at the muzzle. `attack` fires the heavy shot.
- **ice_mite** (56x40): a small banded-chitin scuttler with glowing faceted ice crystals on its back (its shell). In `break` the crystals snap off to dark stubs, with shards on the floor.
- **void_eel** (108x64): a floating serpent. The body is a swept tube that shades from its own normal, with a travelling-wave idle, a rippling dorsal fin with a glowing edge, a pulsing lateral line, a large hinged-jaw head and barbels. `special` rears up and dives into the floor with a violet splash; the Director plays it just before the stage sinks the eel for Submerge.
- **rime_golem** (96x96): a Meridian cargo loader buried in rime. It knuckle-walks on ice-block fists, has crystal spikes on its back, a frosted sodium lamp for an eye, a hazard-striped chest and a cyan core. `special` is Frost Shell (the crystals grow and glints run over it). `break` snaps the armour off and drops it to a kneel.
- **salvage_bot** (80x64): an 80-year-old rusted bot on treads, in faded teal Meridian paint, with a flickering sodium camera eye, a faded Ringborn prayer ribbon on its antenna and a buzz-saw arm. `special` is the self-repair: a top-mounted torch welds its own hull with an arc and sparks.

Every art has a 4-frame idle with secondary motion, plus `attack`, `hurt` and `break`; four of the five also have `special`. Frame counts are 10 to 12. Normal and emissive maps are generated by the registry's sheet builder. Each art also has tuned 24x24 turn-order icon framing.

prologue/enemyart.js also exports the bestiary toolkit (the POC ramps and the spark, sparks, smoke, steam, spot, flame, muzzleFlash, polar, along, limbPlate and dome helpers). The shoals file reuses it, and the CA-beta and CA-gamma bestiary tasks can too.

I checked all five in preview-enemies next to the POC drone at desktop and phone size, and lit in real battle stages. Those were the POC cryo, engineering, corridor and bridge arenas, at desktop and phone, through preview-battle. No encounters or Shoals arenas exist yet (C1 and C3 have not written their data), so the arena shots swap enemy sheets in place with `stage.transform` and pose frames with `sprite.setFrame`. `npm test` passes 217 of 217, scriptcheck is ok, and the dev and minified builds of preview-enemies, preview-battle and main succeed. All strict runs had 0 page errors and 0 console errors.

## C9-alpha

C9-alpha is done. All 11 tracks and all 66 sfx pass the offline bench, with 0 failures and 0 console errors under --strict. `npm test` is green: 229 tests, 217 pass, 0 fail, 12 skipped; the skips are motif checks waiting on tracks from later sub-waves.

Engine changes (TECH_PLAN 9):
- `audio.music(track, { fade, restart })`: `restart` replays a track that is already playing, and `fade` sets the crossfade time (`cs.music(null, { fade: 1.5 })` fades to silence).
- Stings: a track marked `once` (sting_chapter) plays through a duck node, so the current track drops to about -16 dB while it plays and comes back over 1.4 s. `audio.track` is never changed by a sting, and a new sting always plays (it cuts the previous one).
- A malformed track fails alone: `console.error` names it, it is skipped, and everything else keeps playing. This also covers a track that throws while playing.
- `export function compileTrack(def, name)` and `export const MOTIFS` import in node; nothing touches WebAudio at module level.
- Additions: time signatures (3/4, 6/8, 7/8; bpm counts the dotted quarter in 6/8), four new synths (music box, formant choir, sub pulse, swell), and `findMotif` to check where a motif appears in a track.

Tracks (all compose with real progressions and A/B sections; a scratch checker found no clashing notes except brief passing notes):
- **driftmarket**: G Mixolydian, 92, 6/8 jig on plucked harp with frame drum and shaker. Bells take a minor B strain and a descant, then a quiet interlude rings the Ringborn motif slowly.
- **shoals**: C# minor, 70. Glass bells, sub pulses and a cold pad; the B strain is the ice "singing" (a gliding sine voice).
- **meridian**: F# Phrygian, 60. Low drone on an F# pedal, cracked bells, a distant hull clang and a faint pulsing beacon. The bells try the Ringborn motif, break off, then play it slowly.
- **lullaby**: F major, 3/4, 76. HALCYON's lullaby on a music box; the second verse adds harp, a bell descant and a walking bass.
- **warden**: F minor, 3/4, 72. The same lullaby in the minor on a music box that sags slightly in pitch, over a soft "oo" choir and a far cathedral bell; the choir sings the theme the second time.
- **sting_chapter**: a 4.6 s swell into a strike on D add9, timed to land as the card title appears (~1.2 s).

Motifs: `MOTIFS = { lullaby, ringborn, warden, choir }`. The WARDEN theme is the lullaby in the parallel minor, and the Choir motif is a chord.

27 new sfx, calibrated against the existing ones: big hits like `transform` and `awaken` sit with `break`/`encounter`, and quiet UI sounds like `page`, `pod_flip` and `skip` sit with `cursor`. `choir` is soft because it plays on every WARDEN dialog box.

Two existing tracks needed a level change to meet the RMS acceptance: boss gain 0.6 to 0.49 and victory 0.82 to 1.01. Their notes are unchanged.

The bench now renders each track's intro plus one whole loop, draws a piano roll per track with motif markers, and takes `?tracks=`, `?sfx=` and `?stems=` filters. `?stems=` renders each line solo, which I used to balance the mixes against the original title and explore tracks. I also checked the real-time path in headless Chromium: the sting keeps the wanted track, restart works, and a failing track is dropped by name while the next one plays.

### Known issues
- I can't hear the output. Musical quality is checked only through measured levels, per-line levels, spectrograms, piano rolls and a scratch checker for clashing notes. A human listen is recommended, especially the warden choir timbre and the shoals singing-ice glides.
- The RMS acceptance is unweighted, so bass dominates it. The new tracks keep the original tracks' balance: bass about 10-12 dB above the melody in RMS terms, e.g. lullaby measured like title.
- One-beat passing notes that form a major-7th colour against the chord remain on purpose (1 in lullaby, 5 in warden). The checker reports 0 harsh minor-2nd clashes in any new track.

- The lullaby motif's melodic shape (up a minor 3rd, up a 4th, down a half step, down a step, up a step) also turns up by coincidence in victory's B section. Nothing requires its absence, so the tests are unaffected.
- A sting reuses the echo delay time of whatever track is playing, so its echoes follow that track's tempo. This is a minor effect.
